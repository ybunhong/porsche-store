Uncompromising motorsport aesthetics

The Streamline Series captures the thrill of motorsport. Inspired by the aerodynamics of the Porsche 911 GT3 RS, the lightweight titanium frames combine technological sophistication with sporty elegance and maximum precision. A special design highlight are the bars, which take up the striking design language of the rear spoiler and thus impressively underline the dynamism of the series.

A revolution of eyewear experience

The Carbon Fiber series sets new standards in the field of eyewear, combining innovative materials with technologies from motorsport. These carbon frames offer exceptional performance with maximum stability and corrosion resistance at minimal weight. The three-component temple completes the design, striking a balance between flexibility and comfort.

A tribute to the 911

The design of the Flowing Titanium series is inspired by the unmistakable shoulder line and silhouette of the Porsche 911. The 3D temples produce a precise contour edge that creates an exciting interplay of light and shadow.
